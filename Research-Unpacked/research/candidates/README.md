# research/candidates/

Leads promoted from `research/inbox/` that are actively being checked
against their primary research source. Studies here are typically
`verification_status: YELLOW` — plausible, but not yet confirmed against the
primary paper (or confirmed but not yet fully scored).

Nothing in this folder is ranking-eligible. Once verification against the
primary source is complete and the study is fully scored, move the record to
`research/verified/` (if `GREEN`) or `research/rejected/` (if `RED`).
